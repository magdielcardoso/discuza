# Support

## How to Get Help

If you need help with Discuza, have questions, or want to discuss features, here are the ways you can reach out:

1.  **GitHub Issues:** For bug reports and feature requests, please use the [GitHub Issues tracker](https://github.com/magdielcardoso/discuza/issues). Check existing issues first to see if your question or problem has already been addressed.

2.  **WhatsApp Group:** For general discussion, questions, and community interaction, join our [WhatsApp group: Discuza 💬 StackLab](https://chat.whatsapp.com/KpKcKRDUajV6t1UcjpHwyG).

3.  **Email:** For specific inquiries or if other channels are not suitable, you can contact the maintainers directly at [contato@stacklab.digital].

## Community Support

Please remember that Discuza is an open-source project maintained by volunteers. While we strive to be responsive, support is provided on a best-effort basis.

When asking for help, please provide as much detail as possible, including:
*   A clear description of the issue or question.
*   Steps to reproduce (if reporting a bug).
*   Relevant environment details (e.g., browser version, operating system).
*   Screenshots (if applicable).

Being clear and concise helps the community assist you more effectively.
